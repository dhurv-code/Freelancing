Our story

A little corner of Italy in London SE1

We're an Italian deli and kitchen under one roof. Browse artisan cooking ingredients, cheeses and charcuterie in store, then sit down to a plate of handmade pasta, a toasted panino, or simply a perfect espresso.

  • Homemade pasta, made fresh
  • Italian cheeses and cured meats, sliced to order
  • Vegetarian and healthy options on every menu
  • Breakfast, lunch, dinner, dessert and catering
