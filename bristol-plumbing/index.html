Burst pipe? Do this first.

  1. Turn off the stopcockUsually under the kitchen sink or where the mains pipe enters the house. Turn it clockwise until it stops.
  2. Keep water and electrics apartIf water is near sockets or the fuse board, switch off at the consumer unit. Do not touch wet appliances.
  3. Switch off heating, then drainTurn off the boiler and immersion heater, then open the cold taps to empty the pipes.
  4. Contain it and take photosCatch drips, move belongings, and photograph the damage for your insurer. Then call us.

Straight answers about pumps, heating and leaks

How fast can you get to a leak?
Phone us first and tell us how bad it is. Active leaks and homes with no heating or hot water go to the front of the queue, and we will give you an arrival time on the call. While you wait, turn off the stopcock using the steps above.
Do you quote before starting work?
Yes. We find the cause, explain it, and give you a price before we start. If we uncover something new while working, we stop and talk to you before anything is added.
My boiler pressure keeps dropping. Why?
Most sealed heating systems sit around 1 to 1.5 bar when cold. If it falls below 1 bar again after topping up, water is escaping somewhere: a leaking radiator valve, a weeping pump seal, a faulty expansion vessel or a failing pressure relief valve. Topping up over and over hides the fault and adds fresh water that brings in scale and oxygen. Call us and we will trace it.
Do I need a shower pump or a booster pump?
A shower pump boosts a single shower on a tank-fed system. A booster set serves the whole house, usually when mains or stored water arrives too slowly. The right choice depends on your flow in litres per minute and your pipe size, so we measure before we recommend anything.
Is a heat pump right for my home?
It depends on your insulation, your radiators, and space for the outdoor unit and a hot water cylinder. Heat pumps run at lower flow temperatures, often 35 to 50 °C, so some homes need larger radiators or better insulation to stay comfortable. Grants such as the Boiler Upgrade Scheme may be available in England and Wales, and the rules change, so check gov.uk or ask us during a survey.
My radiators are cold or my pump is noisy. What is wrong?
A cold patch at the top of a radiator usually means trapped air, and bleeding it helps. Cold at the bottom points to sludge. A pump that hums, rattles or will not start is often a worn or seized motor. Many circulator pumps can be replaced like for like once the fault is confirmed.
Does hard water affect my plumbing in Bristol?
Bristol is a hard water area. Limescale builds up in taps, shower heads and kettles, and inside boilers and heat exchangers, which makes them work harder and fail sooner. A scale inhibitor, a softener or regular descaling can help. Ask us which suits your home.
How do I check a gas engineer is registered?
Anyone working on gas appliances in the UK must be on the Gas Safe Register. Ask to see their ID card and check the licence number at gassaferegister.co.uk.
